What it does

Built for people who love to drive.

Live stats while you drive, a full recap when you park, and a reason to take the long way home.

01 Drive tracking

Hit the road. It’s already recording.

Turn on auto-start and Trip360 begins a drive as soon as you set off, then keeps going with your phone in your pocket and the screen locked.

  • A live speedometer with speed, distance, duration and altitude
  • Your route drawn on the map as you go
  • A recap of every drive: top speed, speed bands, braking, corners and elevation
Auto-startRecording began
02 Ranks

Climb the board.

Every eligible drive counts toward your place. See where you stand in your country and against the world.

  • Rank by distance, longest drive and more
  • Today, this week, this month or all time
  • Add friends with a code, start a club, compete together
Position 3World · All time
03 Play map

Own the roads you drive.

The map is split into 250 m hexes. Every road you drive claims the hexes it passes through. Take ground from other drivers, defend your own.

  • Hold the most in view and become King of the area
  • Play solo, with your club, or explore your own map
  • Top stealers, gainers and losers, updated as people drive

04 Replay & reels

Relive it. Then post it.

Fly back over any drive on a 3D satellite replay, then turn it into a reel made for Instagram and TikTok, or a story-ready card with your route and numbers.

  • 3D satellite replay of every drive
  • One tap to Make my reel, built on your phone
  • Share cards and a clear stats sticker for your own photos
